Origin

2014, Thiruvarur

Nizhal Trust began when a small group of engineering graduates returned to their home district and found the village well running dry for the second summer running. They stayed six months, understood why the recharge had failed, and rebuilt it with the villagers themselves.

That decision — stay long enough to understand, then build with, not for — became our founding principle.

Mission

What we're here to do

To work alongside rural Tamil Nadu communities so education, water and health stop depending on whether an NGO happens to be around — and start belonging to the village itself.

Journey

Eleven years, one village at a time

2014

First borewell recharge project in Needamangalam block — the trust's founding work.

2016

First after-school learning centre opens, run by local youth volunteers.

2019

Public health camps begin, in partnership with a district government hospital.

2022

Reach crosses 30 villages; trust registers as a formal charitable organisation.

2026

46 villages, three districts, and village-level committees running programmes independently.

What we do

Three roots, one canopy

We work in the areas villages tell us matter most.

Root 01

Kalvi Kudil

24 after-school learning centres, library corners, and scholarships for first-generation learners.

Root 02

Neer Payanam

Borewell rehabilitation, rainwater harvesting, and water-testing camps run with local volunteers.

Root 03

Nalam Kaakum

Monthly health camps, maternal care support, and nutrition programme for children under six.

Why "Nizhal"?

In Tamil, nizhal means shade — the cool, shared ground under a banyan where a whole village gathers to talk, rest and decide together. We don't arrive with answers; we sit in the shade the village already trusts, and build from there.
